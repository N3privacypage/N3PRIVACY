# N3 Bot Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026

N3 is a private Discord bot. It is run by the owner of the official N3 Discord server, for that server only. It is not a public bot.

## What N3 collects

- Discord account information that Discord provides to bots: your user ID, username, display name and avatar.
- Your roles and membership in the N3 server.
- Moderation and security records about the N3 server, such as actions taken by or against an account.
- Information you give N3 when you use its commands or buttons.

N3 does not collect your email address, phone number, IP address, presence or activity.

## How it is used

Only to run the N3 server's security, moderation and community features. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

## Sharing

N3 does not sell, rent or share your information. The only exceptions are reporting violations of Discord's Terms of Service to Discord, and cases where the law requires disclosure.

## Storage and retention

Information is stored on a private server, and only N3 server management can access it. It is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above. Security and moderation records may be kept longer to protect the server.

## Your choices

- You can stop N3 from collecting new information about you by leaving the N3 server.
- You can ask for your information to be deleted through the official N3 Discord server's staff support structure. We may keep security and moderation records where needed to protect the server.

## Changes

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

N3 is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord.
